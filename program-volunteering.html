Volunteering System

A structured 50-hour volunteering experience aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals — transforming students into ready, skilled professionals.

50 Hours Required
5 Weeks
Onsite & Remote

Program Overview

Our Volunteering System is a 50-hour structured program spanning 5 weeks, available both on-site and remotely. Volunteers are placed at the heart of our organization's mission — contributing directly to initiatives aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) that define our mandate.

Each volunteer is assigned meaningful responsibilities, receives professional guidance, and gains real-world experience that builds the foundation for a successful career. This is not passive volunteering — it is active professional development.

Professional Transformation: After completing 50 hours, students emerge fully equipped with the skills, experience, and confidence to enter the professional world — with an enhanced CV supported by our global ambassador network.

SDG Focus Areas

Our volunteering efforts are aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals that guide our organization's mandate:

SDG 4 Quality Education
SDG 8 Decent Work & Growth
SDG 17 Partnerships

What You Will Gain

  • Professional Work Experience
  • Leadership & Responsibility
  • CV Enhancement
  • SDG-Aligned Project Experience
  • Mentorship & Guidance
  • Networking with NGOs & INGOs
  • Ambassador Network Access
  • Career Readiness
The Outcome

Upon completion, volunteers are transformed — they possess all the necessary skills, hands-on experience, and a professionally enhanced CV. Through our ambassador network, they gain visibility and access to opportunities that launch their professional careers.

Quick Facts
