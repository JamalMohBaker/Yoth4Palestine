Who We Are

About Youth For Palestine

A youth-led non-profit organization dedicated to unlocking the potential of Palestinian youth.

We are a Non-profit organization focused on empowering students through education, leadership, communication, entrepreneurship, and real-world opportunities. Our programmes aim to bridge the gap between traditional education and professional development by connecting youth with training, mentorship, organizations, and international opportunities that support their personal, academic, and professional growth.

What we simply will aim for, reaching for more than 1M youth network and alumni by 2035, where they will be one of our graduates entering to the professional stage with a phenomenal outcomes.

Our Mission

Equip Palestinian youth with practical skills and global opportunities.

Our Vision

1 million empowered youth leading change by 2035.

Our Values

Integrity, empowerment, inclusivity, and community-driven impact.

The Challenge

Why We Exist

Youth in our communities have hidden talents — but face real barriers.

Limited Access to Practical Education

Many students lack training in leadership, communication, and entrepreneurship beyond traditional classrooms.

Weak Connection to Opportunities

Students struggle to access NGOs, scholarships, and international programs that can shape their future.

Lack of Professional Experience

Talented youth lack mentorship and real-world exposure to develop and showcase their full potential.

Out of Scope

  • Large-scale funding support

Our Mandate

Focus Areas

SDG 4

Education

Providing trainings and practical programmes that connect students with trainers, ambassadors, and opportunities in communication, public speaking, diplomacy, entrepreneurship, project management, academic support and international competitions alongside certification and recognition systems.

SDG 8

Empowerment

Creating a bridge between education and real life by connecting students with NGOs, INGOs, entrepreneurship opportunities and international forums to transform students into active leaders and contributors within their communities.

SDG 17

Humanitarian Interventions

Supporting youth through humanitarian and environmental initiatives in cooperation with INGOs and UN entities, including crisis response, psychosocial support, education in emergencies, and assistance for conflict-affected communities.
