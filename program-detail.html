Public Speaking & Diplomacy Simulation

An immersive English-speaking environment designed to build the soft skills and strategic thinking today's youth need to lead with confidence.

24 Hours Total
3 Weeks
Onsite

Program Overview

This intensive program focuses on critical areas that many young people lack access to — blending soft-skill development with hands-on practice in a fully English-speaking environment. Students are immersed in real-world simulations that push them to think critically, communicate powerfully, and lead effectively.

The program runs for 3 weeks, totaling 24 hours of immersive, on-site training. Every session is conducted entirely in English, creating natural pressure that accelerates fluency and builds the confidence to speak professionally in any setting.

Immersive English Environment: All sessions, discussions, and simulations are conducted in English — placing students in a high-expectation setting where fluency becomes a necessity, not an option.

Skills You Will Gain

  • Soft Skills
  • Critical Analysis
  • Critical Thinking
  • Project Management
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Building Your Own Business
  • Negotiation & Persuasion
  • Communication Skills
  • Confidence Building
